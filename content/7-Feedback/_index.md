### General Evaluation

**1. Working environment**  
I found the working environment very friendly, sociable, and lively, with a strong spirit of mutual support. It was great to receive comments and feedback on my mistakes.

**2. Support from mentors / team admins**  
The mentors gave very detailed guidance, explained things clearly, and always created opportunities for me to learn and find my own solutions to the problems I faced.

**3. Alignment between the work and my field of study**  
The work I did was related to my field of study at university. This helped me develop my expertise in the area of cloud computing.

**4. Opportunities to learn & develop skills**  
During the internship, I learned many new skills such as using project management tools, teamwork skills, and how to communicate professionally in a company environment.

**5. Culture & team spirit**  
The company culture is very positive: everyone respects one another and works seriously while still staying cheerful. When there was an urgent project, everyone tried hard and supported each other regardless of position. This made me feel like part of the team, even as just an intern.

**6. Policies / benefits for interns**  
The company was supportive and offered flexible working hours when needed. In addition, being able to join events with more experienced colleagues also helped boost my learning spirit, communication skills, and related knowledge.

### Some other questions
- I gained new knowledge about AWS and interned in a one-of-a-kind environment that is sociable and has a strong learning spirit. What I valued most during the learning process was "discipline and perseverance."
- If I had to offer some feedback, I think it would just be the facilities — it was quite crowded, so I didn't get to go to the office very often.
- I would recommend this internship to my friends, because interning here is all benefit and no downside: you get to know many new services, and it also helps a lot for future work.


### Suggestions & aspirations
- I would like to continue interning here in the future. Little by little, with perseverance, I believe I can become an expert in this field.
